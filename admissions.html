The 4-Step Admission Journey

A seamless, merit-driven enrolment process designed with clarity and personal counseling for parents.

01

Online Enquiry

Submit the form below or visit our Central Admissions Bureau opposite Kamma Hostel, Ravipadu Road.

02

OVEST / Marks Review

Appear for the OVEST scholarship examination or submit your 10th Class (SSC/CBSE/ICSE) marks sheet.

03

Stream & Bhavan Allocation

1:1 counseling with our Academic Directors to select between MPC Super-60, BiPC Medical, and choose residential Bhavans.

04

Admission Confirmed

Receipt of official admission kit, academic schedule, uniform measurements, and student hostel room allotment.

Scholarship Examination

OVEST 2026: Oxford VIT Entrance & Scholarship Test

Conducted annually to identify and empower extraordinary young scientific minds across Andhra Pradesh. Performance in OVEST guarantees direct admission into the Super-60 competitive batch and tuition waivers up to 100% funded by the KSR Charitable Trust.

TEST FORMAT
Objective MCQ Pattern
Maths/Bio, Physics, Chemistry
ELIGIBILITY
