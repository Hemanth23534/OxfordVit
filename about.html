Our Heritage & Character

Thirty Years of Scholastic Excellence in Narasaraopet

Founded on the bedrock of moral discipline, mathematical clarity, and student-first mentorship, Oxford VIT has educated over 25,000 scholars who now serve as leaders in medicine, engineering, civil services, and academia.

👁️

Our Vision

To be the benchmark pre-university institution in India where intellectual ambition is nurtured with ethical integrity, academic rigor, and compassionate personal care.

🎯

Our Mission

To deliver precision pedagogy that demystifies competitive examinations, eradicates student anxiety, and guarantees equitable access to premier national institutes through the KSR Charitable Trust.

💡

Educational Philosophy

Concept before calculation. Problem-solving before memorization. We train minds to think critically from foundational principles, instilling life-long academic resilience.

Milestones of Progress

Three Decades of Growth & Institutional Impact

1994 • THE FOUNDATION

Establishment of Oxford Junior College

Founded in Narasaraopet under the visionary patronage of Dr. S. Francis Reddy and K. Sambasiva Rao (KSR) to deliver disciplined, high-quality intermediate education in Palnadu.

2002 • RESIDENTIAL EXPANSION

Balaji Bhavan Super-60 Campus Inaugurated

Creation of the specialized Super-60 competitive residential wing in Reddy Nagar with round-the-clock faculty mentorship for IIT-JEE aspirants.

2010 • STEM RESEARCH PIONEERING

Raman Bhavan STEM & Olympiad Hub

Named in honour of Sir C.V. Raman, establishing advanced physics and chemistry research laboratories, astronomical demonstrations, and national Olympiad training.

2016 • DEDICATED WOMEN'S ACADEMY

Yashoda Bhavan Girls Campus Opened

Providing a completely secured, serene gated residential campus with resident women superintendents and medical care, fostering state toppers in BiPC and MPC.

2021 • GOVERNANCE & PHILANTHROPY

KSR Towers Administrative Headquarters

Centralized executive directorate established in Arundelpet, expanding the KSR Charitable Trust endowment fund to ₹1.5Cr+ for annual rural student scholarships.

2026 • DIGITAL UNIFICATION

The Living Campus Interactive Platform

Unification of all six verified campuses into one centralized digital institutional portal powered by modern 3D WebGL and editorial creative engineering.

OXFORD VIT Junior College & Academy

One centralized educational institution representing all verified campuses across Narasaraopet. Under the benevolent patronage of the KSR Charitable Trust.
