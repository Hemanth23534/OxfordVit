Our Legacy & Philosophy

An Educational Sanctuary Rooted in Discipline and Vision

Founded with an unwavering mission to empower students of Andhra Pradesh and beyond, Oxford VIT Junior College & Academy blends classical academic rigor with contemporary scientific discovery.

For three decades, Oxford VIT has operated as a single, unified beacon of educational integrity in Narasaraopet. Under the guidance of our leadership team and the benevolent patronage of the KSR Charitable Trust, we cultivate leaders in engineering, medicine, and public service.

We reject one-size-fits-all education. Instead, our modular multi-campus system allows each student to thrive in dedicated learning environments—from the intensive competitive pods of Balaji Bhavan to the serene, secure residential environment of Yashoda Bhavan.

✓

Integrated Two-Year Curriculum

Harmonized delivery of BIEAP Intermediate Board syllabus and national entrance exams (JEE, NEET, EAPCET).

✓

Unrivaled IIT & NIT Faculty

Mentors with 15–30 years of classroom mastery who provide daily micro-doubt resolution and error analysis.

✓

Holistic Residential Care

Hygienic steam-cooked nutrition, 24/7 security, resident doctors, and daily yoga sessions for peak mental well-being.

Philanthropic Anchor

The KSR Charitable Trust

Established by Administrative Director K. Sambasiva Rao, the KSR Charitable Trust ensures that financial constraints never stand in the way of brilliance. Every year, the trust provides full and partial tuition waivers, textbook scholarships, and lodging grants to meritorious rural students across Palnadu and coastal Andhra.
