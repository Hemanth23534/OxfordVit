Annual Honors Tradition

Prathibha Puraskaralu & KSR Merit Endowments

Each year, Oxford VIT and the KSR Charitable Trust host the prestigious Prathibha Puraskaralu ceremony. Over 250 deserving rural scholars and high-achievers receive 100% tuition scholarships, laptops, and cash felicitations presented by Administrative Director K. Srinivasa Rao (KSR) and Principal & Academic Director Panadi Raghava.

KSR Scholarship Endowments

  • 100% Free Education Quota: For rural government school toppers entering Intermediate MPC / BiPC.
  • AIR Top 500 JEE Advanced: Full sponsorship of engineering tuition endowments.
  • Government Medical College MBBS Selections: Annual medical kit and stethoscope honors.
  • District 1st IPE Marks: Gold medal and cash award instituted by Dr. S. Francis Reddy.
