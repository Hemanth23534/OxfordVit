Campus Ecosystem

A Second Home Built on Care & Discipline

At Oxford VIT, academic excellence is anchored in residential serenity. With separate dedicated hostels for boys and girls, hygienic steam kitchen dining, supervised sports, and 24/7 medical oversight, students flourish in body, mind, and spirit.

The Residential Bhavans Dining & Nutrition Sports & Wellness
Residential Accommodation

Dedicated Residential Hostels

Distinct, secure campuses engineered specifically for uninterrupted academic focus and tranquil rest.

Boys Residential Super-60 Hub

Balaji Bhavan

📍 Reddy Nagar / Vengala Reddy Nagar, Narasaraopet

Home to our elite IIT-JEE Super-60 boys cohort. Designed with wide cross-ventilated dormitories, individual ergonomic study desks, round-the-clock power backup, silent study halls, and resident faculty available for doubt resolution till 10:30 PM.

Bhavan Amenities:
  • 24/7 CCTV surveillance & dedicated resident wardens
  • Night floodlit volleyball & cricket nets on campus grounds
  • Dedicated hot water solar heating systems
  • Daily laundry services & room housekeeping
Girls Residential NEET Medical Hub

Yashoda Bhavan

📍 Ramireddypeta / Reddy Nagar, Narasaraopet

A premier, highly secure sanctuary exclusively for girl scholars. Supervised round-the-clock by senior female wardens and resident lady lecturers. Features serene landscaped courtyards, spacious air-cooled study rooms, and an attached infirmary.

Bhavan Amenities:
  • Multi-tier biometric security & perimeter electronic surveillance
  • Indoor badminton court and yoga pavilion inside the campus
  • Resident lady doctor and 24/7 emergency vehicle standby
  • Strict parent calling hours & peaceful learning atmosphere
Culinary Hygiene & Health

Steam Kitchen & Wholesome Nutrition

A healthy mind requires pure, energizing nutrition. Food is prepared with love and medical-grade hygiene.

🍲

Steam Cooking Plant

State-of-the-art industrial stainless-steel boilers and steam kettles ensure zero soot, retain maximum vitamins and minerals, and maintain uniform cooking temperature across all meal preparations.

💧

Multi-Stage RO Plant

Centralized commercial Reverse Osmosis (RO) filtration plants installed across every Bhavan provide pure, mineral-balanced drinking water tested weekly for TDS, microbiological safety, and pH standards.

🥛

Daily Dairy & Protein

Sourced directly from certified regional dairy farms. Pure milk served every morning and night, fresh buttermilk served with lunch, and protein-rich pulses, sprouts, and paneer rotated daily.

🩺

Nutritional Supervision

Weekly rotating menus curated by qualified clinical dietitians to sustain mental alertness during study hours without lethargy or digestion issues. Special diet provided during exam weeks.

Sports, Yoga & Emotional Well-Being

Balancing the rigorous demands of competitive exams with vitality, play, and psychological peace.

Outdoor Athletics

Sports Facilities & Courts

Daily 4:30 PM to 5:00 PM recreation allows students to burn off mental fatigue. Our campus grounds feature:

  • Full-size sand volleyball courts with evening floodlights
  • Cricket nets with practice bowling turf
  • Badminton courts with anti-skid surfaces
  • Indoor recreation rooms with tournament carrom boards & chess tables
  • Annual Inter-Bhavan Sports Trophy held every winter
Mindfulness & Healthcare

Yoga, Healthcare & Mentorship

  • Monthly youth motivation addresses by Panadi Raghava
  • 24/7 campus infirmary with full-time resident nursing staff
  • Tie-ups with premier multi-speciality hospitals in Narasaraopet
  • Dedicated student counselors for exam stress mitigation
